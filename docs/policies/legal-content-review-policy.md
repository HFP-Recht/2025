# Legal Content Review Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all modules and block content containing legal concepts, references, or legal claims.

## Mandatory workflow

1. Draft creation (`draft`).
2. Legal/education review (`review`).
3. Reviewer approval (`approved`).
4. Publication by authorized role (`published`).

No direct `draft -> published` transition is allowed.

## Required review checklist

- Legal references present for legal claims.
- No contradictions between questions and model solutions.
- Question-to-solution mapping complete.
- Language level matches target audience.
- Ambiguous wording flagged and resolved.

## Roles

- Author/editor: creates and updates drafts.
- Reviewer: verifies legal and pedagogical quality.
- Admin: publishes approved versions and manages rollback.

## Evidence and audit

- Each publish action is logged in `auditLogs`.
- Reviewer, timestamp, and module version are stored with publication metadata.
